vibeinn is Abhinav's personal clothing brand storefront, started 18 Oct 2026 at `~/passion-projects/vibeinn/`.
The look and flow follow arxstudios.in: header with search, login and cart; promo banner; collection grid with size, type and stock filters; product page; slide-out cart drawer; newsletter block; policy and service links in the footer.

**Why:** Keeps the target in one place so every session builds toward the same store.
