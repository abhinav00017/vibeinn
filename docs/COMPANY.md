# vibeinn company rules

Last changed: 19 Oct 2026. Owner: Abhinav.

## What vibeinn is

A clothing label. We print artwork by hand on blank garments, to order. Each piece is a blank the buyer chooses plus the artwork they pick for the back, the front, or both.

## What we believe

1. **The print is the product.** The garment is a canvas. Spend the money on ink, screens, and artwork, not on logos.
2. **One of a kind.** Hand printing varies. We say so, and we do not hide it.
3. **Small and honest.** We tell the buyer the stock, the lead time, and the rules. No fake urgency.
4. **Quiet brand, loud prints.** See `brand/BRAND.md`.

## How we work

- **Decide in writing.** Every decision goes into `.claude/memory/decisions.md` with the date.
- **One source of truth for the catalogue.** Products, artworks, collections, size charts, and policies live as files in `content/`. Change the file, not the site.
- **Ship small.** One change, one commit, one deploy. The live site is always the latest `main`.
- **Commit as the personal identity.** `Abhinav Naidu Chintala <abhinavch53@gmail.com>`. Never the work email.
- **Keep company work out.** This project does not touch Automate Accounts, its repos, tools, or rules.

## Who does what

Today: Abhinav does everything, with Claude building the site and drafting artwork and copy. Printing, packing, and shipping are by hand.

## Money

- Prices include tax. Shipping is added at checkout.
- Payments provider: not decided. See `.claude/memory/decisions.md`.
- Keep the price list in the product files. Do not quote prices anywhere else.

## Customer promises

These match the policy pages in `content/policies/` and must stay in sync with them.

- Orders leave within 3 working days of payment. Printed to order can take up to 5.
- Delivery in India takes 3 to 7 working days after dispatch.
- No returns for change of mind. Size exchanges within 7 days, unworn, tags on.
- Damaged or wrong item: replaced or refunded on photos, within 7 days of delivery.
- We collect only what the order needs. We do not sell data.
